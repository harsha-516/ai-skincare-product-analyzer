## Sources considered and rejected

- **Sephora US catalogue** (used in the mid-term prototype): real data, but not sold in India and priced in USD.
- **Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Nykaa prices**: their terms prohibit automated collection. The site links to a search for each product on these stores instead. Live prices need their official partner APIs (Amazon PA-API, Flipkart Affiliate API).
- **Open Beauty Facts (India)**: few products with ingredient lists, no prices.
