Skin type, sensitivity, the concerns that bother you most, your budget and any ingredient you avoid. A photo cannot tell us any of this, so the questionnaire comes first.

Hard rules filter first

Out of budget, wrong category, or contains something you avoid? The product is removed before scoring, and no high score can bring it back.

03

What is left is scored on your concerns, preferred ingredients, skin type and budget. Every result tells you, in plain words, why it is there.

Sensitivity comes first

Mark your skin as highly sensitive and fragrance, essential oils and drying alcohols are filtered out by default. Strong actives come with a patch-test reminder.

Ingredient flags you can trust

Fragrance allergens, comedogenic ratings, fungal-acne triggers and pregnancy cautions come from published, dermatologist-reviewed ingredient data, not from marketing copy.

SkinAnalyzer gives informational guidance, not a medical diagnosis. If your skin is painful, infected, bleeding or not improving, please see a dermatologist.
