How your answers are used

Hard rules first. Category, budget and the ingredients you avoid remove products before any scoring.

Then a weighted score. 35% concern match, 25% preferred ingredients, 20% skin-type fit, 20% budget fit.

Then reasons. Each result lists what matched, and anything to watch out for.
