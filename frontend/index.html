FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Try the analysis. It takes about two minutes and needs no account.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. SkinAnalyzer gives informational guidance based on ingredient lists and your answers. If your skin is painful, infected, bleeding or not improving, please see a dermatologist.

What happens to my selfie?

The photo is checked in your browser for a single face, sharp focus, even lighting and enough resolution. It is never uploaded or stored. Estimating skin characteristics from photos is in development, so results today use your answers only.

How is the match score calculated?

Products that break any of your rules are removed first. The rest get a weighted score: 35% concern match (which ingredients target your concerns), 25% preferred ingredients, 20% skin-type fit and 20% budget fit. Parts you leave blank are skipped and the rest are rebalanced.

Where do the prices come from?

From each brand's official Indian online store, on the date shown on the product page. Prices and offers change, so the "Buy" button always opens the brand's live page.

Why don't you show Amazon or Flipkart prices?

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho and Nykaa don't allow other sites to collect their prices automatically. Every product has "Compare on" buttons that search those stores for it, so you can check in one tap.

Do brands pay to be recommended?

No. There are no sponsored placements and no affiliate links. The ranking only uses your answers and the product's ingredients, price and labels.
